A Special Message for You

On this special day, I want you to know how much of a blessing you are to each and every person who gets the chance to cross paths with you. I like you for who you are—not because you are a perfect person, but because you try your hardest to become a better one every day. I admire the person you are, the person you are becoming, and even the parts of yourself that you may not always be proud of. I hope that one day, I'll get to know your darkest secrets—not so that I can judge you or ignore you, but so that I can understand you, accept you completely, help you through whatever you may be carrying, and stand beside you when things get difficult. You don't always have to be strong around me. You don't have to hide the parts of yourself that you think are too much or too difficult to understand. I hope that someday you'll feel safe enough to let me see all of you, the good and the bad, and know that I'll still be here. I hope life gives you all the happiness, peace, and love that you deserve. And whenever you doubt yourself, I hope you remember that there is someone who believes in you more than you realize. Happy birthday. I hope this year brings you closer to the person you want to become, and gives you countless reasons to smile. And no matter where life takes us, I hope you'll always remember that you were, and will always be, someone very special to me.

Today, we celebrate you – your kindness, your beauty, your amazing spirit, and all the wonderful things that make you uniquely you.

Happy Birthday, my Esther! Here's to many more years of laughter, love and beautiful memories. 💕
